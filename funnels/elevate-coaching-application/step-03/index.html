WHAT TO EXPECT

A Focused Conversation About Your Goals, Challenges, and Next Step

The discovery call is an exploratory conversation based on your submitted application and current business situation.

It is intended to clarify your needs, answer general questions, and discuss whether an Elevate Academy option may be suitable.

The discovery call is not a full coaching session and does not guarantee acceptance or enrollment.

  1. 01

    Introductions and Context

    Begin with a brief overview of your business, current stage, and reason for applying.

    Prepare: Be ready to explain what your business currently offers and who you serve.

  2. 02

    Current Challenge

    Discuss the main challenge affecting your progress, client acquisition, offer, systems, or decision-making.

    Prepare: Choose one primary challenge rather than listing every issue in the business.

  3. 03

    Goals and Priorities

    Explain the result or improvement you want to work toward and why it matters now.

    Prepare: Describe a realistic priority that coaching could help you clarify or address.

  4. 04

    Support and Program Fit

    Discuss whether One-on-One Coaching, Group Coaching, or the Self-Paced Course may align with your preferred way of learning.

    Prepare: Consider the level of guidance, interaction, accountability, and independence you need.

  5. 05

    Possible Next Step

    Review the possible next action based on the conversation, fit, readiness, and program availability.

    Prepare: Prepare questions about expectations, participation, and the enrollment process.

BEFORE YOUR CALL

Prepare These Details Before Booking

  • Your primary business goal
  • The challenge creating the most difficulty
  • What you have already tried
  • Your preferred program
  • The type of support you need
  • Questions about coaching expectations
  • Your realistic availability and readiness

What the Discovery Call Does Not Guarantee

  • Acceptance into One-on-One Coaching
  • Enrollment in Group Coaching
  • Immediate program availability
  • A specific business recommendation
  • Clients, revenue, income, or financial results

Any possible next step depends on fit, readiness, availability, and the information discussed during the call.
