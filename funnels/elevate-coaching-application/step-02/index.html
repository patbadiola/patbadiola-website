WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Your Application Is the First Step in the Coaching Process

The application provides context for a discovery conversation and helps determine whether coaching may be appropriate for your current situation.

The final next step depends on fit, availability, program requirements, and the information you submitted.

01

Your Application Is Recorded

Your contact information and application responses are stored in the Elevate Academy CRM for review and follow-up.

  • Your submitted application is connected to your contact record
  • Your preferred program is recorded
  • Your business situation and goals provide context
02

Your Information Helps Determine Fit

Your responses help identify whether your preferred program may match your current goals and support needs.

  • One-on-One Coaching may suit focused individual support
  • Group Coaching may suit guided group learning and accountability
  • The Self-Paced Course may suit independent structured learning
03

You May Book a Discovery Call

The discovery call provides an opportunity to discuss your application, goals, challenges, expectations, and possible next step.

  • Review your application before the call
  • Prepare your main questions
  • Be ready to discuss your current priorities
  • Booking does not guarantee acceptance
04

A Coaching Decision Is Made

After the application and discovery conversation, the appropriate next step may be discussed based on fit and availability.

  • You may be invited to a suitable coaching option
  • You may be directed to the Self-Paced Course
  • You may be advised that coaching is not currently the best fit
  • No outcome is guaranteed
