One-on-One Coaching

FOCUSED PERSONALIZED SUPPORT

Designed for applicants who want focused guidance based on their specific business situation, offer, goals, and challenges.

Best suited for:

  • Business owners who want direct discussion and individualized support
  • People working through a specific strategic challenge
  • Applicants who prefer private and focused conversations
  • People who want support tailored to their situation

What to expect:

  • A discovery call before enrollment
  • Focused conversations about your goals and challenges
  • Recommendations based on the information you provide
  • Active participation and implementation between sessions

Group Coaching

GUIDED LEARNING AND SHARED ACCOUNTABILITY

Designed for applicants who want guided discussion, structured support, shared learning, and accountability alongside other participants.

Best suited for:

  • People who learn well through group discussion
  • Business owners who value shared perspectives
  • Applicants who want structure and accountability
  • People comfortable discussing general challenges in a group setting

What to expect:

  • A discovery or qualification process before enrollment
  • Facilitated group discussion
  • Shared learning and participant perspectives
  • Independent implementation between sessions
