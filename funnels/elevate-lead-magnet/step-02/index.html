USE YOUR CHECKLIST

Turn the Resource Into a Simple Action Plan

The checklist will be most useful when you apply it to your current client-acquisition process instead of trying to complete every improvement at once.

Start with one priority, take one practical action, and review your progress consistently.

01

Review Your Current Process

Compare the checklist with how you currently attract, organize, and follow up with prospects.

  • Identify how new prospects currently find you
  • Review how you explain your offer
  • Check where you store and manage new leads

Complete this step when you can clearly describe your current process.

02

Choose One Priority

Select the checklist step that could create the most meaningful improvement in your business right now.

  • Look for the biggest gap or point of confusion
  • Choose one improvement that is realistic to complete
  • Avoid changing every part of the process at once

Complete this step when you have selected one clear priority.

03

Build Consistency

Turn the selected improvement into a simple routine that you can repeat and measure.

  • Decide what action you will complete each week
  • Create a basic follow-up schedule
  • Review what is working and adjust when needed

Complete this step when the action has a clear schedule or process.

A PRACTICAL REMINDER

Progress comes from applying the right action consistently. The checklist is designed to help you find the next useful step—not overwhelm you with a complicated system.
