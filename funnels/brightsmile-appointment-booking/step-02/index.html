What happens next

We’ll Help You Prepare for Your Visit

BrightSmile Dental will send the information you need before your scheduled appointment.

  1. 1

    Check Your Confirmation

    Review the confirmation sent to your email or phone. It will include the selected dental service, appointment date, time, and assigned dentist.

  2. 2

    Complete New-Patient Information

    If you are a new patient, you will receive a link to complete the New Patient Information survey before your appointment.

  3. 3

    Watch for Reminders

    Appointment reminders will be sent before your scheduled visit so you can review your booking details.

Before visiting the clinic

  • Arrive approximately 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment.
  • Bring any information or documents requested by the clinic.
  • Contact BrightSmile Dental as soon as possible if you need to reschedule or cancel.

Specific preparation instructions may vary depending on the selected service. Follow any instructions sent directly by the clinic.
