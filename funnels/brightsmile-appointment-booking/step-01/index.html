Need help booking?

Can’t Find a Suitable Appointment?

Send us an appointment request with your preferred schedule and dental concern. Our receptionist will review your request and contact you with the next available option.

Request an appointment View available appointments

Submitting a request does not guarantee that the preferred date or time will be available.

Use the appointment request form when:

  • Your preferred time is unavailable
  • You are unsure which service to select
  • You need help choosing the appropriate dentist
  • You have questions before booking

Before your appointment

What to Expect After Booking

After you select your service and appointment time, BrightSmile Dental will help you prepare for your scheduled visit.

Appointment confirmation

You will receive your appointment details by email or SMS after completing the booking.

New-patient information

New patients will receive a link to complete the New Patient Information survey before their visit.

Appointment reminders

BrightSmile Dental will send appointment reminders before the scheduled visit.

Please contact the clinic as soon as possible when you need to reschedule or cancel.

Appointment availability and changes remain subject to the clinic’s current schedule.
