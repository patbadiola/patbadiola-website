WHY YOUR FEEDBACK MATTERS

Honest Feedback Creates a Better Program Experience

Feedback helps Elevate Academy understand how participants experience the program from enrollment through completion.

Your responses can identify strengths, clarify confusing areas, improve support, and guide future updates to the learning experience.

Improve Program Content

Feedback helps identify which lessons, exercises, and explanations were useful and which areas may need additional clarity.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

A participant may identify a topic that needs a clearer explanation or a more practical example.

Strengthen Participant Support

Responses help identify where participants need more guidance, communication, or assistance.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Feedback may reveal where instructions, onboarding, or support information could be easier to follow.

Understand Participant Results

Feedback helps document the types of improvements, insights, or progress participants experienced.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

A participant may describe greater clarity, improved organization, or a more consistent business process.

Guide Future Improvements

Patterns across participant responses can help prioritize future updates and program decisions.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Repeated feedback may identify one area that should be improved before adding more content or features.

How Your Responses Will Be Used

  • To review the overall participant experience
  • To identify useful and unclear parts of the program
  • To improve future content, communication, and support
  • To follow up when a participant requests contact
  • To review optional testimonial permission separately

Your feedback may be reviewed for program improvement and participant support. Feedback will not be used publicly as a testimonial unless you separately provide permission.

HONEST FEEDBACK IS WELCOME

You are not required to provide a positive rating or testimonial. Please share what accurately reflects your experience.

  • Be specific when possible
  • Separate program feedback from urgent support concerns
  • Only provide testimonial permission when you are comfortable doing so

Use the support or contact channel for unresolved access, billing, or urgent account concerns instead of relying only on the feedback survey.
