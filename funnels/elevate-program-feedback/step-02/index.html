WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Your Feedback Becomes Part of the Program Review Process

Submitted responses may be reviewed to identify useful program elements, recurring challenges, support needs, and opportunities for future improvement.

Not every suggestion will result in an immediate change, but repeated and specific feedback can help identify meaningful priorities.

01

Feedback Is Recorded

Your responses are stored with your contact and program information for internal review and participant-support purposes.

  • The submitted program is recorded
  • Your responses remain connected to your contact record
  • Optional testimonial permission remains separate
02

Responses Are Reviewed

Feedback may be reviewed to understand the participant experience and identify common themes.

  • Useful parts of the program can be recognized
  • Unclear content may be identified
  • Support or communication gaps may be reviewed
03

Follow-Up May Be Requested

Elevate Academy may contact you when your responses indicate that clarification or participant support may be helpful.

  • Contact may occur when follow-up is appropriate
  • A reply is not guaranteed for every submission
  • Urgent concerns should be sent through the support channel
04

Improvements May Be Prioritized

Patterns across participant feedback may help guide future updates to content, onboarding, communication, or support.

  • Repeated feedback may reveal a priority
  • Changes depend on relevance and available resources
  • Feedback does not guarantee a specific update

TESTIMONIAL PERMISSION

Public Use Requires Separate Permission

Submitting program feedback does not automatically authorize Elevate Academy to publish your comments as a testimonial.

  • Testimonial permission must be selected separately
  • Identifying information should only be used with permission
  • Feedback may still be reviewed privately for program improvement
  • Permission may be clarified before any public use

When testimonial permission is not provided, the feedback should remain for internal review and participant support only.
