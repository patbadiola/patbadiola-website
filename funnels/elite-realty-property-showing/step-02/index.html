Prepare for your visit

A few things to think about before the showing

You do not need formal documents before booking. Having a few preferences and questions in mind can help make the visit more useful.

  • Rooms and layout that matter most to you
  • Must-have property features
  • Questions about visible condition or improvements
  • Storage and usable-space needs
  • Neighborhood or location questions
  • Your preferred buying timeline
