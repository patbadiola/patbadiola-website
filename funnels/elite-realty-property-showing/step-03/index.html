A few things to consider before the showing

You do not need formal documents for the property visit, but having your preferences and questions ready can help make the walkthrough more productive.

  • Rooms and layout features that matter most
  • Must-have property features
  • Questions about visible condition or maintenance
  • Storage and usable-space needs
  • Outdoor areas and surroundings
  • Neighborhood and location questions
  • Your preferred buying timeline
  • Any concerns you want to discuss with Ethan
