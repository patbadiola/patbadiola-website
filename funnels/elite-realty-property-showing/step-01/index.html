Prepare for your visit

Look beyond the first impression

A property showing is an opportunity to observe how the home feels, functions, and compares with your priorities.

Room layout and flow

Consider how easily you can move between rooms and whether the arrangement supports your daily routines.

Natural light and ventilation

Observe the available daylight, window placement, airflow, and how each space feels during your visit.

Storage and usable space

Review closets, cabinets, utility areas, and other spaces that may support your storage needs.

Visible condition and maintenance

Note visible finishes, fixtures, repairs, wear, and maintenance questions you may want to discuss.

Outdoor areas and surroundings

Consider the yard, exterior spaces, nearby properties, street, and surrounding environment.

Features that match your priorities

Compare the property’s spaces and features with the needs and preferences most important to you.

Bring your questions

Write down anything you would like to ask about the property, neighborhood, disclosures, offer process, or possible next steps.
