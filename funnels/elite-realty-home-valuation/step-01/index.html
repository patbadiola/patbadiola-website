Property and market factors

A home’s market position depends on more than one detail

A useful review considers the property itself together with relevant location, condition, timing, and market information.

  • Location

    Neighborhood, nearby amenities, access, surroundings, and local buyer activity.

  • Property type and size

    Home type, interior size, lot size, layout, bedrooms, bathrooms, and usable space.

  • Condition

    Overall upkeep, visible repairs, systems, finishes, and presentation.

  • Improvements

    Renovations, additions, upgrades, and other changes made to the property.

  • Comparable activity

    Relevant nearby listings, recent sales, and current competition where available.

  • Timing and demand

    Current market activity, buyer interest, available inventory, and the homeowner’s preferred timeline.

Every property is different

The review should be based on the information available for the specific property and should not be presented as a guaranteed sale price or formal appraisal.
