Prepare for your review

A few property details to have in mind

You do not need formal documents before booking, but having a few details available can make the conversation more focused and useful.

  • Recent improvements or renovations
  • Known repairs or condition concerns
  • Approximate property size and features
  • Current occupancy
  • Any unique property characteristics
  • Questions about market position or selling preparation

What to expect from your review

Your intake was submitted

Sophia can review the property information you provided before your scheduled conversation.

The review is personalized

The discussion can focus on the specific home, your questions, and the information currently available.

No obligation to sell

Scheduling a review does not require you to list, market, or sell the property.

Important distinction

The Home Valuation Review is intended for planning and discussion and should not be presented as a formal licensed appraisal or guaranteed selling price.
